Privacy Policy for OpenTermius

Effective Date: 18 November 2026 · App ID: com.opentermius.client · Developer: Wisnu Withanto

OpenTermius is an open-source, self-hostable SSH and SFTP client for Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux under the Apache-2.0 License. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

1. Zero telemetry

OpenTermius never transfers information to external servers unless you explicitly initiate a connection. The app contains no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no crash trackers.

2. Data we process and storage

  • Standalone mode (offline): You can use OpenTermius without an account. Hosts, passwords, private keys, snippets, and tunnel configs are encrypted on your device with WebCrypto (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM) and stay on your device.
  • Self-hosted Gateway mode (optional): When you sign in to your own Gateway URL, the app sends your email, display name, and client-encrypted vault data (AES-256-GCM) to that server only. The server never receives your plaintext credentials.
  • Direct SSH and SFTP connections: OpenTermius connects directly from your device to the SSH host IP and port you enter.

3. Android permissions

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used for SSH/SFTP connections and optional sync with your Gateway.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Keeps active SSH sessions open when you lock the screen or switch apps.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Held only while a terminal tab is open and released immediately when all tabs close.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Shows the ongoing status notification while background SSH sessions are active.

4. Third-party sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user or device data with third parties. All network connections go only to your own SSH servers or your self-hosted Gateway.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: Delete items inside the app anytime, or clear app storage in your OS settings.
  • Gateway data: You or your Gateway administrator control the PostgreSQL database and can permanently delete your account and vaults anytime.

6. Children

OpenTermius is a developer and system administration tool not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Contact and source verification

You can audit the full source code on GitHub or open a question in GitHub Issues.
