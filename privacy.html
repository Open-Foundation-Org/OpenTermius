Privacy Policy for OpenTermius

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 · Application: OpenTermius (com.opentermius.client) · Developer: Wisnu Withanto

OpenTermius is an open-source, self-hostable SSH and SFTP client for Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux licensed under the Apache-2.0 License. This Privacy Policy explains how OpenTermius handles user and device information.

1. Core Privacy Principle (Zero Telemetry)

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it. OpenTermius contains no advertisements, no third-party analytics SDKs, no crash trackers, and no background telemetry.

2. Data We Process & How It Is Stored

  • Standalone Mode (100% Offline Local Storage): By default, you can use OpenTermius in Standalone Mode without creating an account. Your SSH hosts, usernames, passwords, private keys, snippets, and port-forwarding configurations are encrypted locally on your device using WebCrypto (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM) and never leave your device.
  • Self-Hosted Gateway Mode (Optional): If you explicitly configure and sign in to your own self-hosted OpenTermius Gateway URL, the app transmits your account email, display name, and client-side encrypted vault ciphertext (AES-256-GCM) to the Gateway server you specified. The server never receives or stores your plaintext vault master password or unencrypted SSH credentials.
  • Direct SSH & SFTP Connections: When you initiate an SSH or SFTP connection, OpenTermius connects directly from your device to the target SSH server IP/hostname and port that you entered.

3. Device Permissions (Android)

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to establish user-initiated SSH/SFTP connections and optional sync with a user-configured Gateway.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Used only while you have one or more active terminal sessions open so Android does not terminate your live SSH connections when the screen locks or you switch apps.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Held temporarily only while an active SSH terminal session is open and released immediately when all terminal tabs are closed.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display the ongoing foreground service status notification while active SSH sessions are running in the background.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user or device data with third parties. All network connections are initiated solely by the user to their own SSH servers or their own self-hosted Gateway instance.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete individual hosts, keys, snippets, or vaults directly inside the app at any time, or clear all local data by signing out or clearing application storage in your OS settings.
  • Self-Hosted Gateway Data: If you use a self-hosted Gateway, you or your Gateway administrator control the PostgreSQL database and can permanently delete your user account and encrypted vaults at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

OpenTermius is a technical system administration and developer tool not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact & Open-Source Verification

Full source code is publicly auditable at https://github.com/wisnu-w/OpenTermius. If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/wisnu-w/OpenTermius/issues.
